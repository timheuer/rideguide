Ride 01

BrownBoldt

Distance 118 km 74 mi
Elevation gain 1,550 m 5,084 ft
Start From House

Route map

Elevation profile

  • 1,550 m climbing
  • 1,550 m descending
  • 1,578 m high point
  • 448 m low point

Route notes

This is mixed terrain going to the Humboldt FAA tower for the views. Bring water as there are no refill locations along the route after we leave first gravel. It will be single track as we start then on to a paved road until we hit end of pavement. This will turn into groad surfaces and then a paved 5mi climb to the tower. The descent back will follow more downhill pavement back to start.

Route notes

This takes us first up Adero Road before descending into the surrounding terrain. There is bathrooms and water refill at the top of Adero climb. From there we'll 'descend' (maybe some hike-a-bike) into the McDowell area along some fun and challenging terrain. There is an equestrian event this weekend in McDowell so we'll navigate that as best we can. We'll pop back out into Browns for a bit and try to avoid as many sandy washes on the way back home 😁.
